# Останні значки — аркуш 3×2

Прикріпи як зразок стилю аркуш погоди (sheet-farm-f) чи інтерфейсу (sheet-ui-g).

```
Game icon sheet for a cozy mobile farm game, in the same hand-painted cartoon art style and warm lighting as the attached image. Glossy, chunky, cute objects with soft painterly shading and a subtle rim light, clean readable silhouettes, slight 3/4 front view, warm Ukrainian village details. Arrange them in a strict grid of 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, left to right, top to bottom, in the numbered order below. Each object centered in its own cell, fully inside it with a generous white margin, all about the same size. Plain solid pure white background, no cell borders, no shadows on the background, no text, no numbers, no letters.
1 a fluffy warm winter coat with a fur collar and wooden toggle buttons (warm clothes),
2 a knitted winter hat with a big pompom and a red-and-white folk pattern,
3 a cute vintage gramophone with a golden horn and little musical notes coming out (sound on),
4 the same vintage gramophone with a knitted sock pulled over its horn and no notes (sound off),
5 a cup of raspberry tea with a lemon slice and a small thermometer leaning on it (feeling sick),
6 a sleepy little crescent moon wearing a nightcap, resting on a small embroidered pillow (tired, out of energy).
```
