Glossy, chunky, cute objects with soft painterly shading and a subtle rim light, clean readable silhouettes, slight 3/4 front view, warm Ukrainian folk details. Arrange them in a strict grid of 4 columns by 3 rows of equal square cells, left to right, top to bottom, in the numbered order below. Each object centered in its own cell, fully inside it with a generous white margin, all about the same size. Plain solid pure white background, no cell borders, no shadows on the background, no text, no numbers.
```
